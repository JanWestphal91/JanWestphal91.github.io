02 — About

Hi, nice to meet you

I'm into experimental design, art and prototyping. I'm fascinated by how different disciplines interlock to create something new.

I love to draw and tinker with electronics as well as video and audio gear. Graffiti culture is part of my roots – it was my first real connection to illustration and design.

My path wasn't a straight one. Six semesters of communication design gave me a visual foundation, then I worked as a graphic designer for several years – and realised the technology behind it intrigues me just as much. That's why I now study media engineering, bringing both worlds together.

Game development excites me most, because design, code, sound and storytelling come together in one product. My favourites: Oxygen Not Included, Slay the Spire, Diablo and Donkey Kong Country.

I'm married to a wonderful person who brings some order into my chaos, and we have two wonderful kids.
