Zines

Three small printed zines: a short story told in shadows, early verses by Franz Kafka illustrated in black and white, and a wordless boxing fight that ends with skeletons. Each one can be leafed through here as a digital flipbook. The shadow pictures for “Nachts sind die Ratten wach” were photographed from below through glass and paper, lit by a torch from above; a sketch of the setup is shown below the text for that zine.

01 — Nachts sind die Ratten wach

A short illustrated story told in shadows. The pictures were made in a dark room with a camera setup of my own: the camera at the bottom, a glass plate above it with thin paper on top, cut-out shapes on the paper and a torch further up, shining down. Each picture was exposed for 10 seconds. The lettering was drawn letter by letter with a torch in a long exposure.

02 — kühl & hart

Verses from a 1903 letter by Franz Kafka, illustrated in black and white.

03 — Boxkampf

A wordless boxing fight that ends with skeletons.
