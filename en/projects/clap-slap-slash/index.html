Clap, Slap, Slash!

Your mouse is your weapon. The two mouse buttons are your left and right hand, and each one wields its own equipment. Clap, Slap, Slash! is a fantasy roguelite played entirely with the mouse.

The game is in active development. All systems work; the art, the other visuals and the stability are currently being reworked, and more content is on the way. The demo runs about 10 minutes, the full version aims at around 30 minutes per run. The demo can be played on itch.io.

02 — Idea

Your mouse is your weapon. The two mouse buttons are your left and right hand, and each one wields its own equipment. You move the cursor to attack enemies and block at the right moment to parry.

03 — Systems

  • Combinations: the equipment in both hands can be combined, so every run leads to its own combinations.
  • Classes: 3 starting classes with different item pools, each with three starting loadouts.
  • Events: a number of minigames take place inside the events.
  • Meta progression: unlocks and new events between runs. The system is done, the content to unlock is still to come.
