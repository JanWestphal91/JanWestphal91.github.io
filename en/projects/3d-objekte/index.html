3D objects

Models, renderings and scene screenshots from my 3D work. Four models can be rotated right here in the browser: a rigged duck, a player character, a tree and a bridge overgrown with vines. The topology view shows the edges as I modelled them in Blender.

Type
3D
Details
Blender · Modelling

01 — 3D models (04)

Drag to rotate the models. “Topology” shows each model in a neutral material with its edges – only the edges from the model, without the diagonals that appear when the browser splits every quad into two triangles.

3D 01 Duck

Rigged by myself. The feathers are separate objects attached to the rig, so they can be posed individually.

The 3D view needs JavaScript.

3D 02 Player character

Rigged, shown here in the rig's rest pose. Jacket, scarf, hair, belt and boots are separate meshes.

3D 03 Tree

Trunk and roots are one continuous mesh made almost entirely of quads. The crown consists of 15 leaf clusters, bent with a Simple Deform modifier. In the gallery below the tree stands in its scene with lanterns.
