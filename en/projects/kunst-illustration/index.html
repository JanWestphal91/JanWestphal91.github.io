01 — Nachts sind die Ratten wach

A short illustrated story told in shadows. The pictures were made in a dark room with a camera setup of my own: the camera at the bottom, a glass plate above it with thin paper on top, cut-out shapes on the paper and a torch further up, shining down. Each picture was exposed for 10 seconds. The lettering was drawn letter by letter with a torch in a long exposure.
