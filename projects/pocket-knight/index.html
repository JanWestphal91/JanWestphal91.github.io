Pocket Knight

A small pocket-sized device. As you collect steps throughout the day, events are unlocked, leading to different encounters and battles. With each battle, the player gains experience, improves their equipment, and becomes stronger. The events are collected so they can be played one after another in the evening, while the device only needs to be carried during the day.
