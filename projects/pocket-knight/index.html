Pocket Knight

A pocket-sized handheld game console running a turn-based battle game, built from scratch around a small microcontroller, an OLED display and a handful of buttons. The knight and its opponents face off with Attack and Block commands while HP bars tick down on screen, all packed into a hand-soldered protoboard build.
