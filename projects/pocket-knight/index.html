Pocket Knight

A small, pocket-sized device. It is a mini roguelike designed for on the go, where players make progress by walking. The steps collected throughout the day unlock events, which lead to different encounters and battles. With every battle, the player gains experience, improves their equipment, and grows stronger. The events are stored so that players can go through them one by one in the evening at their own pace, while only needing to carry the device with them during the day. Link to the blog post about the development process: <a https://janwestphal.dev/blog/development-process-of-pocket-knight/ </a>
